Our Services

A yatra has a lot of moving parts - permits, vehicles, rooms at 3,000 m, a guide who knows the route. We handle all of it, and tell you exactly what each piece costs.

  • Private Transport

    Innova, Tempo Traveller or bus depending on group size, with drivers who have run the Rishikesh - Kedarnath road for years. Pickup from Haridwar, Rishikesh, Dehradun or Jolly Grant airport.

  • Stays & Camps

    Hotels we have personally checked, plus fixed camps on the trekking routes. Rooms are blocked months ahead, so nobody ends up in whatever is left at 9pm in Sonprayag.

  • Certified Guides

    Local guides trained in high-altitude first aid, on every departure. They carry the oxygen and the oximeter, and they make the call on whether the group goes up.

  • Registration & Permits

    Char Dham Yatra registration and the Uttarakhand e-pass, plus forest permits for Har Ki Dun and Valley of Flowers. Send a photo ID and we file everything.

  • Helicopter & Palki

    Helicopter transfers to Kedarnath from Phata, Sirsi or Guptkashi, and pony or palki arrangements for anyone who should not be walking 16 km. Booked in advance, at the published rate.

  • Medical Support

    Oxygen cylinders, first-aid kits and an evacuation plan on every trip, with a standing arrangement with hospitals in Rudraprayag and Dehradun.
