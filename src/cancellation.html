Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Plans change, and mountains change them more often than most places. This page sets out exactly what you get back, and when.

If you cancel

Cancellation takes effect from the date we receive your request in writing, by email or WhatsApp. The charge is calculated on the total trip cost.

Cancellation charges by notice period
Notice before departure Cancellation charge You receive
More than 30 daysBank transfer charges onlyFull advance refunded
30 to 15 days25% of trip cost75% refunded
14 to 7 days50% of trip cost50% refunded
Less than 7 days100% of trip costNo refund
No-show on departure day100% of trip costNo refund

Helicopter tickets, train and air fares, and government permits are non-refundable once issued, whatever the notice period. Those amounts are deducted before the table above is applied, and we will show you the receipts.

If we cancel

We call off departures for weather, landslides, road closures, government order, or anything else that makes the route unsafe. In those cases you may choose either:

  • Transfer - move to any other departure in the same season at no extra charge, or
  • Refund - receive everything back except costs already paid out and not recoverable from hotels, transport and helicopter operators.

We will always show you what those non-recoverable costs were. In practice they are small when we cancel more than a week out, and larger when a road closes mid-trip.

Trips cut short

If a trip is abandoned after it has started - a road closes behind you, or the group is turned back at a barrier - there is no refund for the days already completed. We will refund unused hotel nights where the property returns the money to us, and we will get you back to the pickup city at no extra cost.

Leaving the group early

If you choose to leave a trip once it has begun, for any reason including altitude sickness or a personal emergency, unused services are not refundable. We will arrange transport down and assistance getting home; those costs are yours.

Changing your dates

A date change more than 30 days before departure is free, subject to availability. Inside 30 days it is treated as a cancellation and a new booking.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the account the payment came from, within 7 to 10 working days of the cancellation being confirmed. We do not issue cash refunds.
